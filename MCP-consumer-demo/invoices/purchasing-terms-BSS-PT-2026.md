# BAY STATE SUPPLY

## Standard Purchasing Terms and Conditions

**Document reference:** BSS-PT-2026
**Effective:** 1 January 2026
**Supersedes:** BSS-PT-2024

These terms govern all purchase orders issued by Bay State Supply, Inc. and apply
to every supplier holding a current pricing agreement, including but not limited
to agreements BSS-HF-2026, BSS-SP-2026, BSS-TM-2026 and BSS-BC-2026. Where these
terms conflict with a supplier's own terms of sale, these terms control.

---

### 1. Definitions

**1.1** "Buyer" means Bay State Supply, Inc., 1150 Providence Highway, Norwood,
Massachusetts 02062.

**1.2** "Supplier" means the party named on the applicable pricing agreement.

**1.3** "Order Date" means the date printed on the face of the purchase order,
being the date on which Buyer issued the order to Supplier.

**1.4** "Pricing Agreement" means the schedule of agreed unit prices executed
between Buyer and Supplier, identified by the reference number shown on each
purchase order.

**1.5** "Receipt Date" means the date on which goods were accepted at Buyer's
receiving dock and recorded against the purchase order.

---

### 2. Orders

**2.1** No order is binding on Buyer unless issued on a numbered Bay State Supply
purchase order.

**2.2** Verbal instructions, email requests and standing arrangements do not
constitute an order and will not be paid.

**2.3** Supplier shall reference the purchase order number on all shipping
documents, packing lists and invoices.

---

### 3. Pricing

**3.1** Unit prices are those set out in the applicable Pricing Agreement.

**3.2** **The price applicable to any purchase order is the price in effect on
the Order Date.** A price that takes effect after the Order Date does not apply
to that order, irrespective of the date on which the goods are shipped, received
or invoiced.

**3.3** Supplier shall give Buyer not less than thirty (30) days' written notice
of any change to agreed prices. A revised Pricing Agreement takes effect on the
date stated in that notice and applies only to purchase orders issued on or after
that date.

**3.4** Buyer is not obliged to accept any price not recorded in a current
Pricing Agreement.

---

### 4. Units of Measure

**4.1** Quantities and unit prices shall be invoiced in the same unit of measure
as stated on the purchase order.

**4.2** Where Supplier's billing system uses a different unit of measure to that
on the purchase order, the invoice shall state the conversion applied and the
resulting extended amount shall be arithmetically equivalent to the purchase
order price for the same goods.

**4.3** A difference in unit of measure does not entitle Supplier to a different
effective price.

---

### 5. Additional Charges

**5.1** Unit prices are inclusive of freight, fuel, energy, handling, packaging,
palletisation and delivery to Buyer's Norwood facility.

**5.2** **No accessorial charge, fuel surcharge, energy surcharge, delivery fee,
minimum order charge or similar charge may be added to an invoice unless it
appears as a separate line item on the purchase order and was agreed in writing
before the goods were shipped.**

**5.3** Charges added in breach of clause 5.2 will be deducted from payment and
Supplier will be notified of the deduction.

**5.4** Buyer will not accept invoices for expedited or premium freight unless
the expediting was requested by Buyer in writing.

---

### 6. Quantities and Receipt

**6.1** Buyer's receiving records are conclusive as to the quantity of goods
received.

**6.2** Where the quantity received is less than the quantity ordered, Supplier
shall invoice the quantity received. The balance may be invoiced when, and only
when, it has been shipped and received.

**6.3** Over-shipments in excess of the ordered quantity may be returned at
Supplier's expense.

---

### 7. Invoicing

**7.1** Supplier shall submit one invoice per purchase order unless partial
shipments have been agreed in advance.

**7.2** Every invoice shall state the purchase order number, the line number,
the item description, the quantity, the unit of measure, the unit price and the
extended amount for each line.

**7.3** **Supplier shall not submit an invoice for charges that have previously
been invoiced.** Re-issuing charges under a new invoice number, a new invoice
date, or as part of a consolidated invoice does not create a new payment
obligation.

**7.4** An invoice which does not reconcile to the purchase order and the
receiving record will be placed on hold and returned to Supplier with the reason
stated. Payment terms do not begin to run until a conforming invoice is received.

---

### 8. Payment

**8.1** Payment terms are those stated on the purchase order, calculated from the
date a conforming invoice is received.

**8.2** Buyer may set off against any sum due to Supplier any amount owed to
Buyer under these terms, including amounts arising from overcharges, unauthorised
charges and duplicate billing.

---

### 9. Disputes

**9.1** Buyer shall notify Supplier of any disputed charge within sixty (60) days
of the invoice date.

**9.2** Supplier shall respond to a disputed charge within fifteen (15) business
days.

**9.3** Undisputed portions of an invoice will be paid in the ordinary course
while a dispute is outstanding.

---

### 10. Notices

**10.1** Notices to Buyer shall be sent to Accounts Payable, Bay State Supply,
1150 Providence Highway, Norwood, Massachusetts 02062.

**10.2** Notices to Supplier shall be sent to the accounts receivable contact
recorded on the applicable Pricing Agreement.

---

### 11. Term

**11.1** These terms remain in effect until superseded by a document issued by
Buyer and identified by a later reference number.

**11.2** Continued acceptance of purchase orders constitutes acceptance of these
terms.

---
